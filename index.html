Catherine (Cathy) Ji

I'm a Physics PhD candidate at Princeton University advised by Benjamin Eysenbach and Gautam Reddy. I'm interested in unsupervised reinforcement learning, applying reinforcement learning to understand the physics of decision-making systems, and estimating and maximizing human agency. I am supported by the NSF GRFP and the Centennial Fellowship. Previously, I did my undergrad at MIT, where I received a B.S. in Physics and Math.
